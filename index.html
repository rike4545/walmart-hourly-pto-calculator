How the estimate works

  • Accrual is calculated from projected service hours (average weekly hours × weeks).
  • Where the chart has annual caps, the calculator subtracts the plan-year earnings you entered.
  • After the PPTO annual earning maximum is reached, the PPTO portion of the earning rate is redirected to PTO, subject to the PTO maximum, as described in the chart.
  • Continuous plans show no annual maximum where the chart says “No max.”
  • The projection holds your current service-year rate constant. If you cross a service anniversary during the projection, actual accrual may differ.

Source: Walmart — Paid Time Off Plans and Earning Rates (Field Walmart, Sam’s, and Supply Chain Hourly Associates; effective 7/1/2024).
